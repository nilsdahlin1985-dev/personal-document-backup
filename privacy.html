Personal Document Backup

Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026.

Scope and operator

Personal Document Backup is a personal rclone configuration operated by the Google account holder on their own computer. This policy describes the intended configuration: read-only access to Google Drive and local exports of selected documents.

Information accessed

The tool requests the Google Drive read-only scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. This permits reading file metadata and downloading or exporting file contents accessible to the authorized account. Metadata may include names, identifiers, folder information, file types, and modification times.

The scope is account-wide; the documents actually selected for backup are determined by the local backup configuration.

Purpose and transfer

Google data is used solely to identify selected documents, export them, and maintain local offline copies and backup history. Transfers use Google's APIs through the locally running rclone program.

This personal configuration does not use Google data for advertising, sale, analytics, AI training, or unrelated purposes. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Exported documents, previous backup copies, and operational logs are stored in locations chosen by the operator. Logs can contain document names, paths, timestamps, and error messages. Google OAuth client credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally by rclone and the operator.

The intended configuration sends document contents directly from Google to the operator's computer. It does not upload them to this website or distribute them to other recipients. Any additional storage sync, sharing, or secondary backup configured by the operator is under their control.

Retention and deletion

Local files and logs remain until removed by the operator or by a retention rule the operator configures. Earlier copies and local copies of documents deleted from Google Drive may remain as part of the backup history.

Control and revocation

The account holder can stop the scheduled task and revoke the app's access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; existing local copies can be deleted separately from the computer and any additional backup locations.

Website hosting

These information pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, or advertising. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process visitor information under the GitHub privacy statement. The information pages do not receive Google account credentials, authorization tokens, or backed-up documents.

Contact and updates

The account holder operating this personal configuration is responsible for its data handling and for updating this policy when the configuration changes. Questions should be directed to that account holder.
